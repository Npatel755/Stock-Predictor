# Video Research Checklist

- [ ] Exact video URL/channel/publish date and access method recorded
- [ ] Authorized transcript? If not, what material was actually viewed?
- [ ] Quotes limited; use original paraphrases and timestamp links
- [ ] Rules extractable without guessing future information?
- [ ] Backtest shown? Full date range, instrument, cost, drawdown and complete trade history disclosed?
- [ ] Promotional conflict or affiliate links?
- [ ] Potential repainting, lookahead, data snooping or screenshot selection?
- [ ] Independent evidence found or not?
- [ ] Tradability on MES/MNQ independently evaluated?
