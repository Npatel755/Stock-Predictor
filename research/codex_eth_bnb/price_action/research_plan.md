1. Sweep/reclaim: price pierces a previous20-bar extreme by0.05ATR, closes back inside with an opposing candle and an extended wick>=40%of its range; relative volume>=1.2. Fade the attempted break; stop beyond the swept extreme. This is an OHLCV pattern, not proof that actual stops/liquidations traded.
2. Failed breakout: a completed close breaks a prior20-bar level by0.1ATR on relative volume>=1.0; within six subsequent bars it closes back inside the old range by0.05ATR. Fade, with stop beyond the observed excursion.
3. Breakout/retest: the same breakout is followed within eight bars by a level retest within0.2ATR; a STRICTLY LATER candle closes beyond the previous bar extreme in the breakout direction. Stop beyond the observed retest extreme. A close0.5ATR through the level invalidates the setup.
4. Opening-range breakout: range07:00–08:00UTC, built from EVERY completed source bar; between08:00 and16:00UTC the first close>=0.1ATR outside, relative volume>=1.0 and on the EMA50 side enters. Maximum one entry per day.07:00UTC is a fixed research convention for24/7 crypto, not a US equity opening or daylight-saving-adjusted exchange session.
5. Opening-range fade: a close breaks that same range, then within four subsequent bars closes back inside by0.05ATR. Fade with excursion stop; at most one event per day. No event before08:00; do not use the unfinished range.
6. Three-candle imbalance retest: completed candle1high<candle3low for bullish zones(mirrored bearish), with middle body>=1ATR and gap width0.2–2ATR. Within twelve LATER bars a touch/rejection of the zone in the displacement direction triggers. A close0.2ATR through the far edge invalidates it. The term sometimes called fair-value gap is a three-candle geometric proxy, not measured fair value or order-book information.
7. Volatility-shock reversal: range>=2.5priorATR, body>=50%range, relative volume>=2 and close in outer25%of range. Within the next three bars an opposite candle closes through the shock midpoint. Fade after confirmation, with stop beyond all observed excursion extrema. No instantaneous fade on the shock bar.
