## Frozen budget before outcomes

- Six strategy families: head-and-shoulders/inverse, double tops/bottoms, converging triangles, bull/bear flags, rectangles, and an equal-priority union of the five mechanisms. No learned mix weights.
- Three horizons:15m,30m,1h. Two fixed recognition settings: responsive(2left/2right pivot confirmation) and selective(3left/3right). Total36specifications×2assets=72primary trials. Flat and simple48-bar momentum on each horizon add12baseline slots. No further grid or threshold tuning.
- Head-and-shoulders requires alternating shoulder/trough/head/trough/shoulder pivots; head prominence, shoulder similarity and time symmetry; prior directional trend; and a CLOSED-bar neckline cross AFTER the last shoulder is confirmed. The inverse pattern mirrors all rules. Recognition at an earlier pivot timestamp is forbidden.
- Double tops/bottoms use confirmed similar extrema, an intervening trough/peak, prior trend and neckline break. Triangles require at least3confirmed high and3low contacts, locally fitted converging boundaries and a close outside a boundary. Rectangles require2high and2low contacts with similar levels. Flags require a completed12-bar impulse followed by8completed consolidation bars, limited retracement and breakout in the impulse direction.
- Fixed geometry: responsive shoulder/extrema tolerance1.25ATR, head prominence0.25ATR; selective0.75ATR/0.5ATR. Pattern span<=96bars; post-confirmation breakout window<=24bars. Neckline/boundary break buffer0.05ATR. Head/valley targets use measured pattern height; stops beyond right shoulder/range edge by0.25ATR, at least1ATR from signal close and at most6ATR. Expected target move must be>=30bp and >=0.5R. These are explicit test definitions, not universal textbook standards.
- Signals persist until opposite pattern, structural stop/target at completed close, or24bars after breakout. A distinct confirmed formation can produce only ONE successful entry; no repeated stopped-out reentries to manufacture activity. Overlapping flag signals have12-bar direction-specific cooldown. Mix conflicts in opposite directions produce no new trade.
- Execution uses a local copy of the vetted engine, changed only for setup identifiers, supplied structural brackets and calendar-correct daily statistics. Unit parity tests must reproduce parent results when these extra fields are absent. Qty/tick, mark-price, funding-jitter and depth caveats remain.
