# Portfolio Construction

## Objective
Improve robustness using genuinely complementary sources of return, not multiply exposure to one hidden bet.

## Procedure
1. Input only eligible strategy return streams from a comparable, properly timestamped research process and net-cost accounting; align sessions/currencies/position horizons carefully.
2. Inspect correlation of trade/event returns, co-drawdowns, exposure overlap, same-direction Nasdaq/S&P beta and loss clustering. Treat NQ+MNQ and ES+MES as closely related underlying risks.
3. Evaluate equal risk, conservative fixed risk weights and shrinkage covariance approaches before flexible mean-variance optimizers. Fit weights on TRAIN only and control turnover.
4. Cap total simultaneous contracts, notional exposure, portfolio risk-at-stop, daily drawdown, and trailing-account breach risk. Never assume stops cap losses under gaps.
5. Test correlated shock scenarios, volatility jumps, long losing streaks, and simultaneous strategy firing. Report diversification benefit after fees.
6. Reject a portfolio whose apparent gain exists only through validation-period weight fitting or a single strategy dominating.

## Deliverables
Position aggregation policy, exposure ledger, allocation method with fitted-window metadata, joint drawdown stress report, and individual vs combined metrics.
