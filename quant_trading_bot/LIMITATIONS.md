# Limitations and open dependencies

Current override to earlier status paragraphs: the reference transfer experiments now use original one-minute ES/NQ history from 2010–2023 with no fitting-row cap. A frozen MNQ candidate was evaluated once on 2026: $6.50 net profit, $536 close-equity drawdown and 28 trades, with a confidence interval including zero. That period is consumed for model selection; no new strategy may claim an untouched 2026 test. Ubuntu 24.04/Python 3.12 WSL core tests now pass with a separate lock; advanced Linux training, Docker and real-feed operation remain unverified. See MODEL_COMPARISON.md for current measured results.

1. There is no evidence of a dependable deployable trading edge. The user supplied verified actual MES/MNQ and distinct ES/NQ history. Reference transfer studies use ES/NQ through 2023, actual micro 2024–2025 validation, and a single frozen MNQ 2026 test. The final test is consumed. Its $6.50 net and 28 trades are weak evidence despite historical drawdown below $2,000. User settings are 50% consistency and provider DLL off; exact account charges and other contract details remain to be verified.
2. The drive was initially nearly full (roughly 10 MB free during the audit), causing an ENOSPC installation failure. Later free space permitted XGBoost, Optuna, DuckDB and Plotly installation and a separate clean environment. Resource pressure also caused transient combined-test process failures; the final acceptance suites passed. The existing PyTorch build is CPU-only despite detected RTX 4090 hardware. No CUDA training claim is made.
3. Clean locked installation and dependency consistency were verified on Windows Python 3.13 and Ubuntu 24.04 WSL Python 3.12. `requirements-wsl.lock` records the Linux closure. Windows-generated absolute catalog paths need the supplied WSL descriptor translation before reuse. Docker and advanced Linux training remain unverified. Windows advanced tests used the development venv with global packages; unrelated pre-existing NLP conflicts were not modified.
4. OHLC cannot reveal tick sequence, spread/depth, queue position or exact exchange fill times. Limit trade-through and stop-first rules are conservative approximations. Market-impact assumptions are configurable, not measured. Stops and emergency exits assume sufficient fill capacity after adverse costs.
5. The primary campaign evaluates each instrument separately. `portfolio.run_portfolio` now combines synchronized MES/MNQ data into one cash balance and shared hard-risk state, reserves same-bar gross exposure, and rejects implicit timestamp filling. Its unit checks pass; a multi-year joint portfolio advantage has not been established. Independent sleeve leaderboards cannot be summed into one-account compliance.
6. Only 2026 quarterly expiration/roll dates are explicitly sourced; nominal dates outside that range need authoritative overrides. Holiday and early-close logic accepts explicit schedules rather than guessing holidays. A verified real schedule was not acquired; operational eligibility remains false.
7. The 50% Lucid consistency cushion is not guessed. The purchased 50%/DLL-off settings are confirmed; checkout price, independent minimum evaluation days and chosen broker API permissions remain unresolved. A provider DLL amount is irrelevant to the chosen DLL-off setting. The research daily stop is a separate risk choice. Funded scaling/payout helpers do not certify account-contract compliance.
8. Neural and RL training are experimental functional implementations. They are not integrated as eligible winners of the default market campaign. CatBoost, HMM, SHAP, stacking, mixture-of-experts, Kelly sizing and SAC were optional research candidates, not validated methods in this version.
9. The default campaign does not perform a broad hyperparameter search. Nested grid/Optuna and ablation helpers are provided for registered inner-training experiments; any such search increases the trial count and must be disclosed. Stress coverage cannot establish resilience to every future market condition.
10. Final-test reservations provide reproducible single-use protocol governance, not tamperproof external custody. Owner deletion or moving the global store can defeat local guards. Final-test access must be tracked across all future research.
11. Streaming paper now retains bounded online feature state and supports checksummed rotating checkpoints, write-ahead inputs, explicit resume, a deduplicated ledger and a no-arrival feed watchdog. These are tested local process-recovery controls, not fault-tolerant storage or an authenticated broker feed. Checkpoints must be trusted local files. Append-only monitoring may repeat a recovered input; canonical accounting uses ledger sequence IDs. Forward paper evidence and service-level operational review remain outstanding.
12. Statistical diagnostics are sample-limited. Bootstrap requires dependence/weak-stationarity assumptions; IID Sharpe adjustments are only diagnostics. Comparing many correlated choices and undocumented earlier research may leave selection bias beyond recorded trial corrections.
