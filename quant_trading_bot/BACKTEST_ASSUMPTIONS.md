# Execution and accounting assumptions

Contract specifications were checked against official CME pages on 2026-11-06: [MES](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/sp/micro-e-mini-sandp-500.contractSpecs.html), [MNQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/nasdaq/micro-e-mini-nasdaq-100.contractSpecs.html), and the [CME fact card](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/files/cme-micro-e-mini-futures-fact-card.pdf). MES is $5/point, 0.25 tick, $1.25/tick; MNQ is $2/point, 0.25 tick, $0.50/tick. Only outright quarterly contracts are modeled; spread tick rules differ.

Signals use completed bars. An order from close i is first eligible at a subsequent bar open, with at least one bar-index step. The default next-open model assumes zero computational latency at that boundary; extra whole-bar delay is configurable. It cannot reproduce exchange queue position or subsecond latency. Submission/acceptance are recorded separately from fill events, but acceptance has no additional delay in the paper approximation. Intrabar stop/target timestamps identify the candle interval rather than a known tick-time execution.

Market orders pay adverse slippage plus half the configured spread and impact, rounded adversely to the exchange tick. Default per-side fees are $0.65 commission, $0.35 exchange and $0.02 regulatory, plus one-tick spread and one-tick slippage. These are **illustrative unverified research assumptions**, not a broker quote. Entry and exit fees reduce cash; mark-to-market equity uses raw contract prices. Spread/slippage costs are embedded in fills, so gross P&L is gross of fees but already reflects modeled execution prices.

Lucid's [published commission schedule](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11508978-approved-products-and-commissions), checked November 6, 2026, lists $0.50 per side for MES/MNQ without a component breakdown. A separately registered sensitivity study applies that commission as the only explicit cash fee to preselected candidates while retaining spread/slippage assumptions. This scenario does not establish that unlisted pass-through charges are zero. The original higher-cost results remain visible.

Capacity uses the previous completed candle's volume. Entries can partially fill; unfilled remainders are canceled (IOC). Risk exits and stops close the whole position with adverse pricing, without a demonstrated depth constraint: this is a stated limitation and potential adverse-gap underestimation. Limit orders require a one-tick trade-through; a mere touch never fills. Stop-limit orders cannot fill in the trigger candle because sequencing is unknown. Generic matcher order types are broader than the strategy engine, which uses market entries, protective stop exits and trade-through targets.

Scheduled session and maximum-holding exits are known at the open and execute before checking that candle's later high/low. For positions still active, stops are checked before targets in ambiguous candles; opening stop gaps fill at the worse opening reference. This is conservative OHLC handling, not observed path reconstruction. Risk liquidation uses the adverse candle extreme and is explicitly a bound, not a known tick trajectory. Final accounting liquidations use the last close plus adverse costs and are tagged; they are not executable live fills. The engine refuses an open raw position changing contracts without a liquidation quote, preventing roll-gap profit.

Weekday trading clock and 16:40 America/New_York flattening are conservative research defaults. Exchange holidays and early closes require an explicit verified schedule; missing bars are flagged rather than filled. A gap with an open simulated position triggers liquidation at the next known raw open and permanent lockout. Stale/out-of-order streaming data halts paper operation. Real bid/ask, market depth, historical margin changes and exchange liquidation mechanics have not been measured.

The registered 5/15-minute campaigns override the generic flat setting to 16:30 ET, an available bar open before the published cutoff. Reported `exposed_fraction` is bar-close position occupancy: trades opened and closed within one candle can register zero occupancy. It is not measured elapsed exposure; exposure-adjusted statistics based on this proxy need that limitation.
