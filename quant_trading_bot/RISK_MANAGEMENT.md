# Independent risk controls

Research defaults use one MES or MNQ contract, a $75 maximum estimated trade risk, $150 open risk, $300 daily loss circuit breaker, $1,000 maximum drawdown, five consecutive losses, four orders/minute and one correlated contract across equity-index exposure. These are analyst limits, not Lucid account entitlements. Estimated stop risk includes fees and adverse fills; price gaps can still exceed the estimate.

Fixed-dollar sizing floors quantity to integer contracts and never increases size from model confidence. `RiskManager` checks size, estimated loss, open risk, correlated quantity, slippage and order rate independently of predictions. Gross portfolio notional grants no diversification credit to MES/MNQ. `portfolio.run_portfolio` combines synchronized sleeves into one cash balance, one hard-risk state and an optional funded-account state. Beginning-of-bar exposure remains reserved until the entire bar is processed, so an intrabar exit cannot free capacity for another order at the same bar open. Stable instrument order is disclosed as a conservative execution priority. Input timestamps must match exactly; any explicit intersection belongs in the registered study and must disclose exclusions.

The maximum-drawdown kill switch persists across session changes. A daily loss lockout resets only at the next session. Models cannot override either state. Duplicate order IDs, chronological data, integer quantity, tick alignment and broker-position reconciliation are checked. Reconciliation mismatch disconnects the paper broker and cancels pending orders. Critical streaming failures require review; reconnection cannot clear a killed risk state.

Paper shutdown cancels outstanding orders and records open positions. A missing exit quote is not replaced with an invented fill. Historical replay has a separately labeled terminal accounting close. An authorized live provider/broker must eventually supply exchange-native stops, cancellation acknowledgments, rejected-order handling, heartbeat monitoring, emergency flattening and real account reconciliation before a live implementation can exist.

Alternative sizing helpers and experimental RL do not bypass this guard. ATR, volatility targets and Kelly-based leverage were not selected or researched on actual market data; they are intentionally not default production behavior.
