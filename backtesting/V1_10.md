## Change and rules

The only change from v1.9 is completed-hour volume of at least **2.8 times the mean of the preceding 20 completed hours**, up from 2.4. The signal hour is excluded from that mean.

Use standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute candles**, with at least **250 completed hours** for warm-up. Retain the 72-hour channel, EMA250, direction-signed 200bp close/EMA gap, 1.5 hourly ATR(14) initial stop, **5R target**, 3 hourly ATR trail, both directions and weekends. Confirmed-hour signals enter at the next five-minute open. Initial brackets use the signal close; trail changes affect later ticks only.

Keep $50,000 starting capital, $100 cost-aware planned risk, maximum 5x notional leverage, three entries per UTC day and a $300 personal daily entry gate. These personal controls do not certify funded-account compliance.

## Retained weaknesses and uncertainty

The improvement is not uniform. The reset 2025 H1 falls from $2,599.62 to $2,355.45; 2025 H2 falls from $3,543.29 to $3,445.63; and 2026 H2 through October 27 falls from $562.72 to **$474.21**. These reset-account results are not a continuous return and must not be summed into the original baseline.

The already-inspected 2018-2019 spot diagnostic improves from $5,455.36 to **$5,622.07**, summed across 12 independently reset segment accounts. Those sums are not continuous returns, actual leveraged spot fills or perpetual futures performance. They are not added to the baseline, and remain below the earlier v1.4 proxy sum of $6,833.37.

The conditional paired seven-day-block bootstrap interval is **$-1,139.53 to $2,155.99**, including zero. It does not correct for repeated selection. The cumulative search count is **832 configuration slots, including repeated controls**, plus diagnostics. All original 2023-2026 observations are reused selection history; no fresh forward observations have been added. A stronger future edge is not established.

Pine compilation and TradingView trade matching remain unverified. Pine omits Python extra basis-point fill slippage, financing and stop-first ambiguity handling; symbol precision and initialization can also change totals.
