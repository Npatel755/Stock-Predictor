## Rules

Use standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute candles**. Signals use completed hourly candles and need at least 150 hours of warm-up.

1. Buy above the preceding 96 hours' highs while above hourly EMA150; short below the preceding 96 hours' lows while below that EMA.
2. Require completed-hour volume at least **2 times the mean of the preceding 20 completed hours**, excluding the current hour from the denominator.
3. New filter: the signal-hour close must be at least **200bp (2%) beyond EMA150 in the trade's direction**. Long: `(close / EMA150 - 1) * 10000 >= 200`. Short: the negative of that value must be at least 200. This is signed distance, not an absolute-value filter. No EMA slope filter is active.
4. Enter at the next five-minute open after the hour closes. One position at a time; both directions and UTC weekends allowed.
5. Initial stop: 1.5 hourly ATR(14) from the signal close; target: 4.5 times that distance. Brackets are not silently rebased to a future fill.
6. Tighten a 3 hourly ATR trailing stop after each completed five-minute close using the latest completed hourly ATR. Changes affect subsequent ticks.
7. Keep $100 cost-aware planned risk, maximum 5x leverage, three entries per UTC day and a $300 personal daily entry gate. These are personal controls, not funded-account compliance certification.
