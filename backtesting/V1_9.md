## Change and rules

The only parameter change from v1.8 is the initial profit target: **5R instead of 4.5R**. The stronger volume filter from the second experiment is not combined with this change; that combination has not been selected or verified.

Use standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute candles**, with at least **250 completed hours** for warm-up. Retain the 72-hour channel, hourly EMA250, direction-signed 200bp close/EMA gap and completed-hour volume at least 2.4 times the preceding 20 hours' mean, excluding the signal hour. Signals confirm hourly and enter at the next five-minute open.

Initial stop remains 1.5 hourly ATR(14); trailing stop remains 3 hourly ATR. Stops and targets use the signal close. Trailing changes affect subsequent ticks. Both directions and weekends are enabled, one position at a time. Keep $50,000 starting capital, $100 cost-aware planned risk, 5x maximum notional leverage, three entries per UTC day and a $300 personal daily entry gate. Those personal controls do not certify funded-account compliance.

## Selection and verification

Two separately frozen campaigns tested 36 exit geometries and 24 entry-filter combinations. Both used the same exact v1.8 reference; the decision rule required finishing both and choosing the highest-net complete-gate pass. One candidate passed each campaign. The 5R target makes $12,113.22; the alternative volume threshold of 2.8 with the original 4.5R target makes $11,749.94. The 5R result is saved as v1.9; the alternative remains a research candidate.

The gate requires higher main net than the exact saved reference, no worse drawdown, at least 100 entries, no margin calls, positive independently reset 2025/2026 halves with at least six entries each, positive 2026 H1 under 4bp extra slippage, positive earlier 2020-2022 and higher full-period profit under both stressed costs. Fresh replays reproduced the parent, selected main/4bp/8bp results and every gate scenario; reconstructed equity matches the broker. Earlier numbered profiles are unchanged.

The 2020-2022 check makes $7,109.73. The full reset-fold results are retained in the reports. Fold sums are not continuous account returns. The cumulative count is **808 configuration slots, including repeated controls**, plus diagnostics. All original 2023-2026 observations are reused selection history.

## Retained weaknesses

The improvement is not uniform. Independently reset 2026 H1 falls from $554.45 to **$448.72**; H2 through October 27 falls from $763.15 to **$562.72**. The weak H1 remains positive at 4bp extra slippage ($304.34) and in the additional 8bp diagnostic ($38.15), but its cushion is smaller than v1.8's.

The already-inspected 2018-2019 spot proxy falls slightly from $5,462.33 to **$5,455.36**, summed across 12 independently reset segment accounts. These sums are not continuous returns or actual futures fills and are not added to the baseline. The earlier v1.4 performs better on that inspected proxy.

The paired seven-day-block bootstrap interval is **$-1,125.64 to $2,070.23**, including zero. It conditions on already-selected rules and does not correct search bias. There are no new forward observations, no universal historical winner and no established stronger future edge.

Pine compilation and TradingView trade matching remain unverified. Pine omits Python extra basis-point fill slippage, financing and stop-first ambiguity handling; symbol precision and initialization can also change results.
