## Rules

Use standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute candles**. Allow **250 completed hours** for warm-up. Signals use completed hours and enter at the next five-minute open.

1. Break above the preceding **72 hours' highs** for a long, or below their lows for a short.
2. Require the signal-hour close at least **200bp beyond hourly EMA250 in the trade's direction**.
3. Require completed-hour volume at least **2.4 times the mean of the preceding 20 completed hours**, excluding the signal hour.
4. Keep the initial stop at 1.5 hourly ATR(14), target at 4.5R and trailing stop at 3 hourly ATR. Stops/targets are based on the signal close; trail changes affect later ticks only.
5. Keep both directions, weekends, one position at a time, $100 cost-aware risk, 5x maximum notional leverage, three daily entries and a $300 personal daily entry gate. These personal limits do not certify funded-account compliance.

The changes from v1.7 are **96 to 72 hours for the channel**, and **EMA150 to EMA250**. The volume threshold, signed gap, exits and execution assumptions are fixed.
