# ETH v1.3 — independent channel-breakout research

v1.3 is an alternative to DVS, saved separately. v1.2 and its downloads are unchanged. The broader historical replay improved, but **v1.3 lost money in 2026 and did not beat v1.2 over 2023–2026**. It is experimental rather than an established upgrade for funded trading.

## Rules

Use a standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute chart**. Signals use completed hourly candles:

1. Buy when the hourly close exceeds the preceding 80 hours' highs and is above the hourly EMA 200. Sell short below the preceding 80 hours' lows when the close is below that EMA.
2. Submit after the hour's final five-minute candle closes; enter at the next open. One position at a time.
3. Initial stop: 2 hourly ATR(14) from the signal close. Target: three times that initial distance. Brackets are not silently rebased to a future entry price.
4. Trail the stop by 3 hourly ATR from each completed five-minute close, using the latest completed hourly ATR. Stops only tighten; changes affect subsequent ticks.
5. Keep $100 planned risk including estimated round-trip costs, a $300 personal daily entry gate, three entries per UTC day, $50,000 initial capital and at most 5x notional leverage. These personal gates are not a simulation of contractual funded-account rules.

The Python scenario includes 0.04% commission per side, two ticks plus 2bp adverse market/stop slippage, 3.3bp/day financing at six snapshots, and stop-first handling of ambiguous brackets. No live orders are submitted.

The continuous 2020–2026 v1.3 replay has 921 entries, profit factor 1.178 and 3.65% running-peak intrabar drawdown. Its 2023–2026 replay has 494 entries, profit factor 1.155 and 3.94% drawdown. Independently reset annual or half-year rows do not add up to the separate continuous account result. Positions remain marked at endpoints; none remained open in these listed selected replays.

The selected candidate passed the predefined **historical** gate: training-year consistency, both validation halves positive, validation aggregate above v1.2, sufficient entries, no margin calls and positive continuous 2025 at doubled extra slippage. All five 2020–2024 training years were positive. The gate does not establish future performance and does not erase the negative 2026 audit.

## Robustness and selection limits

Continuous 2025 v1.3 P&L was +$2,129.00 at 2bp extra slippage, +$1,879.34 at 4bp and +$1,406.11 at 8bp. The 2026 audit deteriorated from −$1,077.22 to −$1,275.59 and −$1,645.57 under those scenarios. Nearby development-period channel/stop/trailing settings were also profitable, but they reuse historical data and cannot select a replacement for the frozen rule.

Earlier rounds screened 384 DVS candidate slots, then 24 exit-management slots and 12 independent-family slots. This channel follow-up screened another 24: **444 slots including repeats**, with additional diagnostics. The family itself was discovered using the same data. 2020–2022 was first a failed DVS transfer test, then explicitly became development data. 2025 selection was reused; 2026 was already inspected. There is no untouched holdout or future-paper-performance claim.

## TradingView comparison

Open the text file, copy all of it into a new strategy in Pine Editor, save and add it to the five-minute chart. Allow at least 200 hourly candles for signal warm-up. Python initializes from the selected dataset start; TradingView can load a different history length.

The exporter gates current-hour signal values to the confirmed final five-minute candle. Management on earlier candles uses the previous completed hourly ATR. This follows the availability distinction described in [TradingView's higher-timeframe documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/other-timeframes-and-data/), but has not been reconciled against a TradingView signal/trade export.

Pine compilation remains unverified. Pine models fixed tick slippage; it omits Python's extra basis-point fill slippage, financing and stop-first ambiguity policy. Quantity/tick precision and time-exit recalculation also require reconciliation. **Do not expect identical headline P&L between the Python cost scenario and TradingView.** The text file is an experimental signal/management export, not evidence of exact execution parity.

Dashboard: select **v1.3** in **Pine strategy version**, then click **Run v1.3 backtest**. This loads the saved channel strategy in Research mode, locks its preset settings and shows results in **v1.3 backtest**. The **ETH v1.3** tab also provides the download, profile and replay. The **Strategy research** tab shows the saved experiment and ledgers. Missing later candles are rejected; no candles are fabricated.

## Reproduce

From the repository root:

```powershell
.ethvenv/Scripts/python.exe -m backtesting.channel_refine
.ethvenv/Scripts/python.exe -m backtesting.independent_audit backtesting/artifacts/optimization/round4_v1_3_channel_refinement
.ethvenv/Scripts/python.exe -m backtesting.export_independent backtesting/artifacts/optimization/round4_v1_3_channel_refinement
```

Search and audit resume only against matching manifests. The frozen search, ledgers, hashes, chronological folds and stress tests are under `backtesting/artifacts/optimization/round4_v1_3_channel_refinement/`. The like-for-like cold-start 2023–2026 replay is `comparison_2023_2026.json` in that directory. No funded-account deployment has been performed.
