## Rules

Use standard **BINANCE:ETHUSDT.P, five-minute candles**. Signals confirm hourly after at least 150 completed hours of warm-up.

1. Buy when an hourly close exceeds the preceding 96 hours? highs and is above hourly EMA 150. Sell short below the preceding 96 hours? lows when below that EMA.
2. Submit after the hour?s final five-minute candle closes; enter at the next open. One position at a time.
3. Initial stop: 1.5 hourly ATR(14) from the signal close. Target: 4.5 times the initial stop distance. Brackets are not rebased to a future entry price.
4. Trail by 3 hourly ATR from each completed five-minute close using the latest completed hourly ATR. Stops only tighten and changes apply on later ticks.
5. Keep $100 planned risk including estimated round-trip costs, a $300 personal daily entry gate, three entries per UTC day and at most 5x notional leverage. These personal gates do not assess contractual funded-account compliance.
