## Current rules

The completed-daily/4H bias, confirmed pivots, 15m breakout, separate retest/confirmation, daily lock and $100 planned risk remain. Experimental changes are:

1. Target: **4.5:1 reward/risk**, compared with v1.1's 2:1.
2. Stop buffer beyond the retest extreme: **0.4 ATR**, compared with 0.1 ATR.
3. Entry filter: five-minute ATR must be at least **10 basis points (0.10%)** of price.
4. Size includes estimated round-trip commission/slippage in the planned-risk denominator.

The full 2023–2026 research replay has a 27.9% win rate, versus the baseline's 39.9%. This seeks net expectancy rather than higher win rate. Risk and leverage were not increased.

### Earlier-regime test: important failure

Frozen profiles were tested on previously unused 2020–2022 Binance ETH perpetual data. Refined v1.2 made **−$3,093.86**, with **8.55%** modeled running-peak drawdown. It profited in 2020 but lost in 2021 and 2022. No parameters were changed to erase this failed transfer.

See [earlier-regime audit](reports/strategy_transfer_2020_2022.md) and [execution stress](reports/v1_2_execution_stress.md). Improved 2023–2026 results do **not** establish a durable edge across regimes. v1.2 remains experimental, not a validated funded-account strategy.

## Run in Python

From the repository root:

```powershell
.ethvenv/Scripts/python.exe -m backtesting run --mode research --config backtesting/configs/dvs_eth_v1_2.json --end 2026-10-28 --output backtesting/artifacts/dvs/v1_2
```

Dashboard: **B · Research → Research preset → DVS ETH v1.2 (experimental)**. Fixed rules are displayed, manual risk inputs are disabled, and a complete range is selected. Parity mode stays separate. The JSON's `strategy_version: v1.1` identifies the supplied base state machine; extra fields define the v1.2 derivative.

## Execution limits

Python assumes next-open entries, 4bp commission per side, two ticks plus 2bp adverse market/stop slippage, 3.3bp/day financing and pessimistic stop-first handling. Pine implements experimental signals/risk sizing and next-open entries, but fixed-tick slippage omits the extra basis-point fill slippage, financing and pessimistic OHLC policy. These differences are in its header. Pine compilation and export reconciliation remain unverified; exporter tests are not a Pine compiler.

Rejected research confirmations consume the setup in both Python and the regenerated Pine. No live orders were added. Missing October 28 and November 2 candles were not fabricated. Future paper observations and actual execution-feed reconciliation remain necessary.

The refined file is the frozen round-two choice. Separate causal allocation diagnostics compare existing variants with cash; they do not overwrite it or establish independent forward validation.
